Data for the charts lab: shop_monthly.csv

576 rows: 24 months of revenue, units, discounts and customers for 4 regions and 6 departments of a fictional clothing chain. Fictional data for classroom use.

Load it in Google Colab (2 steps)
  1. Press the button below to copy the data.
  2. In a Colab cell, type the first line exactly, paste the data under it, and run the cell:
    %%writefile shop_monthly.csv
(paste here)
Then check with !head -3 shop_monthly.csv.
