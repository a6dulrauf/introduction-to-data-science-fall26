Data for the correlation lab: beach_days.csv

120 fictional summer days: temperature, ice-cream sales, beach visitors and rescue calls. Fictional data for classroom use.

Load it in Google Colab (2 steps)
  1. Press the button below to copy the data.
  2. In a Colab cell, type the first line exactly, paste the data under it, and run the cell:
    %%writefile beach_days.csv
(paste here)
Then check with !head -3 beach_days.csv.
